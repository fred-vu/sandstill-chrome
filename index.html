SandStill

An offline sand art screensaver for Chrome

A quiet moment
in motion.

Choose a pattern. Watch a polished steel ball draw through soft simulated sand. Let the finished artwork rest.

172 built-in patterns

Explore sixteen collections, search for a shape and keep favorites close.

Made for a slower pace

Start at 0.5× speed and pause, restart or move to another drawing whenever you like.

Sound with separate controls

Optional meditation music and a quieter rolling effect, mixed to your preference.

Private by design

Patterns, preferences and simulation run locally. No account, ads or browsing access.

SandStill runs inside a fullscreen window in desktop Chrome. It does not replace your operating system's screensaver or lock screen.
